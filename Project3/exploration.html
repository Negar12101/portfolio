01 · How has the World series changed over time?

Annual temperature anomalies, 1961–2024, relative to 1951–1980. The supplied World aggregate is used directly; no smoothing or averaging across country rows.

02 · How do Iran and Canada compare?

Original annual observations for two illustrative countries. Both lines share the same axes. Missing values create gaps.

03 · How do earlier and recent period averages compare?

Mean annual anomaly in 1961–1980 versus 2005–2024 for six illustrative countries. Each mean requires at least 16 of the 20 annual observations. Countries are ordered by their recent-period mean.

04 · How widely do 2024 anomalies vary across locations?

Histogram of available 2024 anomalies for rows with a three-letter ISO3 code. This operational filter excludes aggregate codes; it includes countries and territories. Each retained location receives equal weight.

05 · When do warmer years appear across selected countries?

One cell per country and year for the same six illustrative countries. Color encodes annual anomaly using a symmetric scale centered on zero. Gray cells indicate missing values.

06 · How complete is the annual record?

Percentage of country and territory rows with a missing annual value, by year. The denominator is the same ISO3-filtered set in every year. Missing values are neither filled nor replaced with zero.

Source: supplied IMF Climate Change Indicators CSV, using FAOSTAT data. All temperature values are anomalies in °C relative to the 1951–1980 baseline, not absolute temperatures. Country selections are illustrative, not a representative sample. Vertical scales differ between charts; read each axis before comparing.
