Uneven Warming

Exploring temperature change across countries, 1961–2024

Negar Ashktorab · Individual project · Instructor approved

Project Overview

This project extends my Project 3 Temperature Change Explorer into an interactive explanation of how warming differs across countries.

The central question is whether countries experience similar temperature changes and how their long-term patterns differ.

I will use annual temperature anomalies relative to the 1951–1980 baseline, covering 1961 through 2024.

The analysis will distinguish sustained trends from year-to-year variability.

Six static visualizations will examine annual patterns, country comparisons, decade averages, and warming rates.

The final website will connect these views through country selection, time filtering, and informative tooltips using D3.

Annotations will explain the baseline, missing values, and the difference between temperature anomalies and absolute temperatures.

The goal is to help a general audience understand why a single summary cannot describe every country's experience.
